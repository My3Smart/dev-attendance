# Smart Attendance

i want to create simple attendance that use nfc card , student touch card at nfc reader and the attendance will be record it will capture student name also if student didnt bring their card their can just enter pin from 4 digit of front ic and 4 digit of back ic examples ic number 000307-02-0031 their just enter 00030031 , so the attendance will be capture and it also record the attendance was by card or pin, also two api file one to call student data from school system , one for whatsapp api , parent will get wahtsapp notifications when their child touch for attendance saying their child arrive at school , if after the time school start the child didnt touch card parent also get notifications saying their child absent
